Medical Disclaimer & Professional Care Guidance

HeelFix is strictly an educational and visual self-tracking application. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment advice, or clinical evaluations.

When to see a doctor: If your heel cracks are deep, painful, bleeding, showing signs of infection (redness, warmth, pus), or if you have diabetes, neuropathy, or poor circulation, consult a qualified podiatrist or healthcare professional immediately.
